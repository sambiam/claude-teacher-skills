# Building Thinking Classrooms task sheet — the durable rows

The community-maintained *BTC Curricular Math Task Sheet K-12*:
`docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1-tJ2DzGJ0JXpmcRJoJxsVBA3d0_2ygxfjH3c1s2GqLM`

**Read this before recommending the sheet.** Checked 1 Sep 2026: 1,051 rows, of
which 819 sit in the bands that matter for Years 7-10. Of those 819, **241 have
no link at all** and **438 link to an individual teacher's Google Doc, Slides or
Drive file** of unknown permission and unknown quality. Only about **82 point at
a durable, publicly maintained resource** — those are the ones below.

So the sheet is a *lead generator*, not a task bank. It is a **B**-grade source:
treat a row as a suggestion to go and look at a named public resource, be
sceptical of the Drive links, and never plan around it unattended. The rows worth
keeping are mirrored here, so you do not need to re-pull it — but if you want to,
`.../gviz/tq?tqx=out:csv` returns the whole sheet (~449 KB) with no auth, filtered
on column B (`Grade Level`). The `/pub?output=csv` form redirects and is not worth
chasing.

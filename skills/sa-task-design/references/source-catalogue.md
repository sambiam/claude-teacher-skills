# Vetted source catalogue

Every source in this catalogue was checked on **1 September 2026**, and what came
out of checking is recorded here. Verdict column: **A** = use freely, durable, free, no login. **B** =
usable with a caveat. **C** = do not depend on it.

Anything marked B or C is why the offline `task-bank-*.md` files exist.

## Your own local library — check it before the web

The highest-value material is usually already on the school's drive, and it needs
no internet at all. **Always look there first**: a downloaded lesson with a
teacher guide and expected student responses beats an equivalent web task every
time.

If your class/topic folder structure is connected to Claude, tell the skill where
these live — or add a `local-assets.md` alongside this file listing them — and it
will search there before reaching for a link.

### Citizen Math (formerly Mathalicious)
Departments that hold the library typically have, per lesson, a **student handout
PDF** and a matching **teacher guide PDF**. The maths topic is usually written
into the handout filename, so the folder is greppable — `ls "<year folder>"`
tells you what is available. The catalogue by topic:

- **Middle primary / Year 6-7 band** — area of shapes (*Ad Vantage*) · central
  tendency (*Overrated*, *Wealth of Nations*) · expressions and variables (*My
  Fellow Americans*) · interpreting graphs (*Downside Up*) · decimals (*Temp
  Work*) · percent (*Scalped*) · rates (*NewTritional Info*) · ratio and rate
  (*Payday*) · ratio (*Jenius*) · scale factor and area (*1600 Pennsylvania*).
- **Year 7 band** — circles and rates (*Pizza Pi*) · circle geometry (*Axle
  Roads*) · linear modelling (*To Have and to Hold*) · order of operations
  (*Common Cents*) · percent and fractions (*Grading Scales of Justice*) ·
  percent sale (*Coupon Clipping*) · probability and percent (*Licensed to Ill*)
  · counting and probability (*PairAlysis*) · rational numbers (*About Time*) ·
  unit rate (*Big Foot Conspiracy*) · ratio and proportion (*Text Me Later*) ·
  one-step equations (*Hair Today Gone Tomorrow*).
- **Year 8 band** — cylinder and sphere volume (*Bottled Water*) · gradient and
  intercept (*Domino Effect*) · line of best fit (*Seeking Shelter*) · linear
  equations, gradients and intercepts (*You're So Fined*) · linear modelling
  (*Opportunities Costs*, *Sweet Tooth*, *Safely Rest*) · **Pythagoras (*See You
  Ladder*)** · scale factor (*Scale Factor*) · volume of a cylinder (*Timber*) ·
  writing and solving linear equations (*Fuel Economy*) · square roots (*Square
  Dancing*).
- **Year 9 band** — central tendency and distribution (*Good Cop Bad Cop*) ·
  exponential decay (*I Remember*) · gradient (*Wage War*) · exponential growth
  (*Billions and Billions*) · linear inequalities (*Datelines*) · supply and
  demand (*WiiBates*) · probability (*Happy Meal*, *Spin City*) · probability
  tables (*Hereby Sentence*) · quadratics (*Connected*, *Out of Left Field*) ·
  intercepts and gradient (*Prescripted*).
- **Year 10 band** — correlation (*Pic Me*, *Win at Any Cost*) · geometric
  analysis with equations (*Canalysis*) · exponential decay (*House of Pain*) ·
  exponential growth (*Pandemic*, *XBOX Xponential*) · periodic and trigonometric
  functions (*Rise and Shine*, *Mercury Rising*) · polynomials (*Second Degree*,
  *Going Once Going Twice*) · probability and statistics (*Bumpy Flight*).

**Running one in a single lesson.** These are designed for two periods. Read the
teacher guide first — it names the natural split point. Run the provocation plus
the first analysis section in lesson 1; the "what should we do about it" argument
section is lesson 2, and it is the part carrying the *Reflective* disposition, so
do not routinely cut it.

⚠ US contexts, US currency, US units in places. Convert or acknowledge —
students will notice, and noticing is fine, but plan for it.

### Three-act media
`three-act-catalogue.md` gives the task, the mathematics and the question for 83
tasks, but the **video and image files live elsewhere** and are the one thing
this skill cannot reconstruct offline. If a 3-act lesson is planned more than a
day out, download the media now into a `<task-name>/` folder on the school drive
and note the path.

### Where new material should go
- Task slides go into the lesson deck in the class's unit folder — this skill's
  primary output.
- A written-up task the class will keep using goes in a `Challenging problems/`
  (or equivalent) folder, filename naming the topic.
- Downloaded 3-act media goes in a per-task folder beside the others.

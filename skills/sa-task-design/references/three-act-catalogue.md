# Three-act task catalogue (offline mirror)

Mirrored from the public *Dan Meyer's Three-Act Math Tasks* sheet
(`docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jXSt_CoDzyDFeJimZxnhgwOVsWkTQEsfqouLWNNC6Z4`),
read 1 Sep 2026. 83 tasks. Most carry a **CC-BY** licence in the sheet's licence
column.

Columns in the source sheet: Task Title · Background Information · Desmos
Activity · Lesson Plan · Act 1 · Act 2 · Act 3 · Sequel · Standards 1–4 ·
Suggested Question · License. An `x` means that asset exists for the task.

**Media lives elsewhere** (101qs.com, the author's blog, gfletchy.com,
tapintoteenminds.com). This file gives you the *task* — title, mathematics and
the question — so you can plan the lesson without the sheet, and know exactly
what to go and download.

## Re-pulling the sheet
`https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jXSt_CoDzyDFeJimZxnhgwOVsWkTQEsfqouLWNNC6Z4/gviz/tq?tqx=out:csv`
returns clean CSV. The `/pub?output=csv` form 302s to a signed googleusercontent
host and is not worth chasing.

## Mapping US standards to SA years
`6.*` → SA Year 7 · `7.*` → SA Year 8 · `8.*` → SA Year 8–9 ·
`F-LE`, `A-CED`, `A-REI` → Year 10 / 10 Methods · `G-SRT`, `G-C`, `G-GMD`,
`G-GPE`, `G-MG` → Year 9–10 · `S-MD`, `S-ID` → Year 10 / 11 Methods.
`MP.4` is "model with mathematics" — it appears on nearly all of them and is the
tell that these meet the SA modelling descriptors.

> The four **"Best …"** tasks are the strongest *Reflective*-disposition tasks in
> the whole catalogue: the class must invent a measure of accuracy before they
> can answer, and defending the measure is the lesson. They need almost no media
> — four hand-drawn attempts on the board will do.
